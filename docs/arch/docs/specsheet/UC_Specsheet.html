PRELIMINARY SPECIFICATIONS

Universal Controller

Modular industrial IoT gateway and controller
Product brief and specifications
November 2026

One controller, many connectivity options. The Universal Controller is a family of DIN-rail modules that click together side by side. Start with the controller and its 24 field inputs and outputs, then add 4G, Wi-Fi + Bluetooth or LoRa only where you need them. Every module also works on its own as a remote node.

Pay only for what you useAdd 4G, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth or LoRa as separate modules instead of buying a fixed router with radios you never switch on.
Real IO, not just a modem4 universal analog inputs, 10 digital inputs and 10 protected outputs on the controller, shared with every module.
Secure at the hardware levelCertified hardware root of trust, signed and encrypted updates, no open services by default.
Keeps working through power cutsEvery module can take a plug-in battery + charger board with an 18650 Li-ion cell, and every module sends a last-gasp alarm when power fails.
Your own LoRa sensor networkThe same LoRa module works as the gateway radio and as the remote node: no third-party gateway needed.
Built for India firstIndia LTE bands, 865–867 MHz LoRa, and Indian certifications planned from day one.

Fieldbus controller

ProcessorArm Cortex-M33, 144 MHz, with hardware crypto, on a removable control card; a 250 MHz control card with TrustZone and PSA Certified Level 3 root of trust is planned
VariantsStandard, or with Ethernet (10/100BASE-TX, RJ45)
Analog inputs4 universal channels, each selectable: 0–10 V, 4–20 mA or PT1000; protected against 24 V mis-wiring. Accuracy ±0.5 % of full scale, ±0.3 % after calibration*
Digital inputs10 × 24 V DC (IEC 61131-2 type 1/3), optically isolated in 2 groups; DI1–DI2 usable as pulse counters
Outputs8 relays, one changeover contact each, 10 A, switching AC or DC, each with a replaceable fuse; 4 fast 24 V high-side outputs with current monitoring, short-circuit protected; a hardware watchdog switches outputs off if the controller stops; status LED on every input and output
Serial1 × RS-485, galvanically isolated, switchable termination and bias; Modbus RTU master / slave
CAN-FD1 × CAN-FD, protected, switchable termination; J1939 and CANopen for vehicles and machines
Data logging128 Mbit flash; at least 1 year of typical logging*; real-time clock with coin-cell backup
Service portUSB-C for commissioning and diagnostics
IndicatorsPower, run, error, bus
