# R34 — LoRa module: part-level draft BOM (pre-schematic)

Status: COMPLETE (first issue, pre-schematic) — lines tagged TBC still need a decision or a check
Date: 2026-11-26
BOM file: `modules/lora/bom/draft-bom.csv` (module repo `UC-HW-LoRa`), 69 lines: 57 main board, 12 module level

## 1. Summary

- **What it is:** a part-level draft of the LoRa module (one 2-layer PCB in a 45 mm DIN housing, 100 units). Every line has a manufacturer part number checked on a manufacturer or distributor page (sources below), a second source where one exists, and tags: `TBC`, `count ESTIMATE`, `per ST reference design`, `per housing fit`.
- **Basis:** the original functional BOM (owner, 2026-11-26: "keep our original bom. need 6 DI's and 4 Analog ins") — STM32WLE5 + TCXO + RF switch, 16 MB NOR, buck 5 V + LDO 3.3 V power stage, external antenna + PCB SMA, 4-pin stack bus, battery socket, RS-485 + 6 DI + 4 AI on fixed terminals, 4 high-side DO on the TPS274160B. No R32 cost-down lever applied.
- **No prices in the CSV** (they go to `bom/prices.csv` once quoted). 100-unit prices seen during verification are in section 4, ESTIMATE only. PTC fuses carry no online price (owner rule: fuses only from local quotes).
- **Departures from the functional list** (all flagged, section 5): RF switch PE4259 instead of ST's obsolete BGS12SN6; LDO TPS7A25 picked; a quad AND gate added for the watchdog gating; a 25 mA current limiter added on each analog input; a VS TVS added on the output supply; stack-bus jumper parts listed. Big open item: the **pin budget** (≈ 42 signals vs 29 I/Os) — an I/O expander is likely and is **not** in the BOM.

## 2. Rules applied

- Owner part rules (`docs/arch/CLAUDE.md`): reliability first; ST/NXP/Nordic/Renesas MCU; no Mornsun; −40…85 °C for active parts; ≥ 5-year longevity; second source where possible; no fine-pitch BGA; easy to place for Indian CMs.
- Owner decision 2026-11-26 (R32): keep TCXO, external antenna + SMA, TPS274160B, 16 MB NOR, 45 mm housing; 6 DI and 4 AI.
- Fixed 3.81 mm screw terminals (the architecture line says "fixed terminals"; the D-34 recommended pluggable flanged set is not used here).
- Passive values and counts are pre-schematic estimates.

## 5. Design observations (not applied — for the schematic and the owner)

1. **The 5 V rail is used.** R32 lever A said the LoRa has no 5 V loads, but the 5 V rail is the meeting point of the buck, the USB-C 5 V input and the battery-board socket (R23 pin plan: 5 V × 2, GND × 2, SDA, SCL, INT_N, DETECT_N). Dropping it would change the battery-board interface. Kept as designed.
2. **Analog reference.** On the UFQFPN48 package VREF+ is bonded to VDDA (R32), so the LM4040 2.5 V reference is read on an ADC channel to correct VDDA, not used as VREF+. Accuracy then rests on calibration; the 0.1 % divider and shunt parts make that calibration stable.
3. **Surge clamp vs part ratings.** SMBJ33A clamps at 53.3 V at full pulse current; the LMR51420 is rated 36 V and the TPS274160 VS is 50 V absolute maximum. R06 flagged the same gap (open point 5). Options (not applied): an over-voltage cut-off before the buck, or a 60 V buck (e.g. TI LMR36006 class), and SMBJ30A/SMBJ28A on VS; settle with the surge level from the test lab.
4. **5 V input.** At a 5 V DC input the buck runs in dropout (≈ 4.5–4.8 V out); the LDO still makes 3.3 V. PWR-5 asks for a front end that works at 5 V — met through the LDO headroom, not a buck-boost.
5. **LDO heat.** 5 V → 3.3 V at 0.2 A is 0.35 W; the TPS7A25 thermal pad needs a copper area on both layers.
6. **Pin budget (main open item).** The STM32WLE5CCU6 has 29 I/Os (DK/RS), of which ≈ 6 are taken by SWD, LSE, VDD_TCXO and BOOT0. Needed: SPI NOR 4, two UARTs with DE 6, RF switch 1, ADC 7 (4 AI, reference, DO current sense, input voltage), DI 6, AI mode 4, op-amp power 1, DO EN 4 + DIAG_EN + SEL/SEH 3, watchdog WDI 1, LED 1, battery-board I²C + INT + DETECT 4 ≈ 42. Shortfall ≈ 19. A 16-bit I²C I/O expander (TI TCA9535 / NXP PCA9555 class, µA standby) on the battery-board I²C would close it; the 73-ball STM32WLE5JC is a fine-pitch BGA (rule 7). **Not added — owner decision.**
7. **USB-C programming.** The STM32WLE5 has no USB peripheral and its ROM bootloader uses UART/SPI, so "programmed through USB-C" (MFG-2, PWR-9) needs a USB-UART bridge chip (not in the functional design, not added) — or USB-C stays power-only on the LoRa and programming uses SWD pads or the stack-bus RS-485 on the test jig. If power-only, the USBLC6-2SC6 can become a single VBUS TVS.
8. **Battery sleep current** (LORA-4): TPS3430 10 µA typ (19 µA max) and the op-amps (40 µA for four) dominate unless switched off; both can be fed from a GPIO in sleep (watchdog disabled via SET pins or powered down, which also forces the outputs off). THVD1420 standby < 1 µA; NOR deep power-down ≈ 1 µA; LDO ≈ 2 µA.
9. **Board orientation.** R23/R24 assume a vertical PCB with the stack-bus headers and the SMA on the top edge; the brief said "one per side wall". Placement only, no part change.
10. **ST reference availability.** st.com timed out from this session; ST's MB1720 / MB1791 BOMs and AN5457 should be downloaded at schematic stage for the exact matching values (the RF passive lines are counts, not values).
